# Picked reference scenes

The nine image `ReferenceBrief` specs preserve the owner's 12 October 2026 image picks from [the decision log](../../../docs/decisions.md#the-look). The day PNGs in [`testing/reference`](../../reference/) are byte-exact copies of the selected candidates. Their hashes and original generation paths are in each spec. No pixels have been corrected or regenerated.

The briefs are the first stage of the [Atlas reference scenes](../../../docs/atlas.md#assets), **not executable `ReferenceScene` instances or renderer targets**. They name the frame each future scene realises, its intended zoom rung, visible composition and the corrections that the build must make. The `rung` is an intended view category, not an approved camera scale. All paths in the JSON are repository-relative.

`OwnerApproved` applies only to the selected image and its composition, density, silhouettes and mood. It does not approve the generated projection, text, materials, brass decoration or other slips. The decision log and Atlas laws still govern, and every scene containing traders inherits the five recorded figure corrections: a non-gold emblem, no jacket close to brass, a curled wrist for a plane throw, no mouth in the shouting pose, and a somewhat larger head for readability at 40 pixels. The character sheets include those corrections explicitly. No 40-pixel readability measurement is claimed here.

Camera values, clock, asset placements and numeric expectations use `{ "_tag": "Pending", "reason": "…" }` until the generators and executable reference scenes make them concrete. The separately scoped material and proportion picks below do not fill these pending scene fields. `themes` names both intended themes; `reference.night` is pending because the owner dropped generated night rounds. Night will be rendered by the Floor. Pending fields must never be interpreted as zero, an empty successful test, an executable scene or a passed comparison.

The current [look harness](../README.md) still captures its labelled synthetic scene and compares it to independent CPU line targets. Generated reference images have no clean line layer and must not replace those targets. Once the real reference scenes exist, migrate these briefs into the Atlas `ReferenceScene` schema, retaining each image hash, source and owner decision, and record renderer baselines separately from image picks.

The original nine-image preservation evidence remains in ignored `work/look/reference-preservation-001/`. The current `bun work/look/reference-preservation-004/verify.ts` checks the exact sixteen-brief/sixteen-image set, retaining the original nine strict assertions, the day/night material checks and the five selected proportion views. The earlier `001`, `002` and `003` verifiers and receipts remain unchanged. It checks byte-exact image copies, dimensions, SHA-256 hashes and the recorded approval boundaries. The first nine briefs still leave renderer fields and night references pending.

The [13 October colour audit](../../../docs/atlas.md#reference-colour-audit--13-october-2026) checked every colour rule against all nine images. Its per-frame build corrections are appended to the briefs. No reference PNG was altered. The owner subsequently approved corrected day material A, with brass and engraving provisional until real instrument geometry exists; the owner subsequently chose the top night option as its own pick.

## Approved day material study

`materials-day.json` is a separate `MaterialReferenceBrief`, not an image-generation `ReferenceBrief` or an executable look-test target. Its byte-exact `testing/reference/materials-day.png` preserves the owner's corrected A choice from 13 October. `approvedVisibleDayValues` stores only the shown day material colours; `lineOpacity` is recorded separately, and brass and engraving live under `provisionalDayInstrument`. Unshown support slots are explicitly unreviewed, and no placeholder night values or obsolete diagnostic aliases are promoted.

The brief records the real capture configuration and hashes for reproduction. That metadata does not approve the camera scale, blockout proportions, instrument geometry, kit unit, interface or renderer acceptance. The owner subsequently picked the top night option derived from these day materials; the day brief’s `night` field links to that separate approval without expanding the day scope.

## Approved night material study

`materials-night.json` preserves the owner’s top-row choice, night B, from the final blind comparison. `testing/reference/materials-night.png` is a byte-exact copy of the picked DPR 1 render. Its 28 `approvedVisibleNightValues` record only the visible material colours, ordinary and dark outlines, and flat lit lamp/screen faces. `flatMaterialMapping` explains collapsed equal-valued slots, and `strokeClasses` distinguishes face-local dark strokes from the complete instrument contour. The pick approves the shown night treatment, not geometry, proportions, the kit unit, lamp or instrument design, an unseen interface or renderer acceptance. Real instruments still need the provisional brass and engraving review. The original nine scene briefs continue to await their own executable night scenes; this material frame does not supply those scenes.

## Approved proportions and kit unit

The five `proportions-*.json` files are `ProportionReferenceBrief` records for the owner's Option 1 (Roomy) choice on 13 October: the day post, floor, cast and native-readability views, plus the night post. Each selected PNG is a byte-exact copy of its shown DPR 1 capture. The owner's note is “it looks better height-wise.”

Approval covers **`K = H/8` and only the nine Roomy ratios** recorded in `approvedRatiosRelativeToH`. Parameter definitions distinguish the head ellipsoid from hair, the shoulder parameter from the sleeve envelope, and nominal station width/depth from actual clearances. The captured construction does not turn its other dimensions into approved values. The readable-size frame uses an 800×240 capture where nominal standing height projects to 40 CSS pixels; inspect at 100%, since a scaled embed does not preserve that display size.

Skin, hair and clothing materials, figure and asset design, eight stations per post, the 36-figure population, physical units and brass remain unapproved. Newly exposed colour bands, additional construction dimensions, camera settings, collision clearances and interface approval are also outside this pick. The original material briefs retain their own scope; they are not broadened or rewritten by the proportion choice. These records are not executable scene targets or Stage 0 approval. The [decision](../../../docs/decisions.md#the-look) and [Atlas values](../../../docs/atlas.md#approved-proportions-and-kit-unit--13-october-2026) are the current dimension authority.
